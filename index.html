Object 01 Fintech / Product Architecture

Cardy

“Thanh toán card đi? Để Cardy!”

Vietnam's credit-card market is fragmented across dozens of commercial banks, each with differing cashback tiers, point multipliers, and merchant partnerships. Consumers frequently leave significant rewards unclaimed simply because matching spending behavior with card rules is non-transparent. Cardy is a recommendation platform that starts from the user's spending goals and determines the optimal card.

01 · Financial Knowledge Bank reward mechanics & merchant fee tiers
02 · User Problem Suboptimal card choices & fragmented rewards
03 · Product Logic Goal-based category mapping & alternative ranking
04 · Technology FastAPI backend + PostgreSQL relational schema
